# Billing, Insurance, and Payment Policies

## What payment methods does Nawaloka accept?

Nawaloka accepts Cash, Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, and direct bank transfers. Cheques are not accepted for OPD services or final inpatient bill settlements.

## What insurance companies have direct billing agreements?

We have direct billing (cashless) agreements with Sri Lanka Insurance, Ceylinco, Softlogic Life, AIA, and Allianz. Pre-authorization is required at admission.

## What is the room charge for a private room?

Standard private rooms cost LKR 15,000 per day. Semi-luxury rooms are LKR 25,000/day, and VIP Suites are LKR 45,000/day. ICU beds are billed at LKR 35,000/day.

## How much does a coronary angiogram cost?

A standard elective coronary angiogram package costs approximately LKR 85,000, which includes the procedure, day-ward stay, and medical supplies.

## What is the financial hardship policy?

Patients demonstrating severe financial hardship can apply for a discount or installment plan via the Medical Social Worker department, subject to management approval.
