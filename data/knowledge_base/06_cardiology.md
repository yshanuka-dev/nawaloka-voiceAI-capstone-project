# Cardiology Department Services and Procedures

## What cardiology services does Nawaloka offer?

Nawaloka Cardiology offers 24/7 emergency PCI, CABG surgery, echocardiograms, stress testing, holter monitoring, pacemaker insertion, and cardiac rehabilitation.

## What is the STEMI door-to-balloon time target?

The target 'door-to-balloon' time for patients arriving with an acute ST-elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI) is less than 90 minutes to ensure rapid reperfusion.

## How does the cardiac rehabilitation programme work?

The 12-week program includes supervised exercise training, dietary counseling, stress management, and medication adherence education for post-MI and post-CABG patients.

## What tests are available in the non-invasive cardiology lab?

The non-invasive lab offers ECG, 2D/3D Transthoracic Echocardiography, Transesophageal Echocardiography (TEE), Treadmill Stress Tests, and 24-hour ambulatory BP/Holter monitoring.

## What is the heart failure management pathway?

The pathway involves multidisciplinary care aimed at optimizing Guideline-Directed Medical Therapy (GDMT), fluid management, patient education on daily weights, and early post-discharge follow-up.
