# Infection Prevention and Control Guidelines

## What is the hand hygiene policy?

Staff must perform hand hygiene following the WHO '5 Moments': before patient contact, before aseptic tasks, after bodily fluid exposure, after patient contact, and after contact with patient surroundings.

## What PPE is required for airborne precautions?

Airborne precautions require an N95 respirator (fit-tested), gloves, gown, and eye protection (goggles or face shield) before entering the patient's negative-pressure isolation room.

## How many negative pressure isolation rooms does Nawaloka have?

Nawaloka Hospital has 12 dedicated negative pressure isolation rooms across the medical wards and ICU for patients with airborne infectious diseases.

## What is the cleaning protocol for patient rooms?

Standard room cleaning uses 1000 ppm hypochlorite solution. Terminal cleaning (after patient discharge) involves a deep clean followed by UV light disinfection or hydrogen peroxide vapor logging.

## What is the needlestick injury protocol?

Immediately wash the wound with soap and water (do not squeeze). Report the incident to the supervisor and Infection Control team immediately. Proceed to ER for post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP) assessment within 2 hours.

## How does the antibiotic stewardship programme work?

The program restricts the use of broad-spectrum antibiotics. Restricted antibiotics require approval from an infectious disease specialist or clinical microbiologist within 48 hours of empirical starting.
