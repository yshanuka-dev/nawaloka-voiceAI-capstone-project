# General Patient FAQs, Visiting Hours, and Hospital Facilities

## What are the opening hours of Nawaloka Hospital?

Nawaloka Hospital's main facility operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — Emergency, inpatient wards, ICU, labour room, and pharmacy never close. OPD (outpatient) clinics run from 07:00 to 22:00 daily; specialist consultant clinics typically run between 14:00 and 21:00 by appointment. Diagnostics (X-ray, CT, MRI, lab) operate 06:00–22:00 with on-call cover at night for emergencies.

## Is the hospital open 24/7?

The Emergency Department, inpatient services, ICU, labour room, and pharmacy are open 24 hours every day including weekends and public holidays. OPD clinics operate 07:00–22:00; specialist consultant clinics run by appointment between 14:00 and 21:00.

## What are the OPD timings?

Outpatient (OPD) clinics are open daily from 07:00 to 22:00. Walk-in OPD consultations are accepted, but appointments are recommended to avoid waiting. Specialist consultants run channelling sessions between 14:00 and 21:00; popular sessions fill up early — please book ahead.

## What are the visiting hours for inpatients?

General ward visiting hours are 11:00–13:00 and 17:00–20:00 daily. ICU and CCU have restricted visiting (15:00–16:00 and 19:00–20:00, two visitors at a time, no children under 12). Maternity ward visiting is 11:00–13:00 and 17:00–19:00. Please carry a valid ID; visitor passes are issued at the main reception.

## When can I visit a patient in the ICU?

ICU visiting hours are strictly 15:00–16:00 and 19:00–20:00 daily. Only two relatives at a time, and children under 12 are not permitted in ICU areas. Please sanitise hands at the entrance and follow the nursing staff's instructions; PPE may be required for certain patients.

## When are the pharmacy and lab open?

The hospital pharmacy is open 24 hours every day. The laboratory operates 06:00–22:00 for routine tests; STAT (urgent) tests and on-call coverage are available around the clock for inpatients and emergencies.

## What should I bring to my appointment at Nawaloka?

Please bring: (1) a government-issued photo ID (NIC, passport or driving licence), (2) your appointment confirmation SMS or booking ID, (3) any current medications you are taking — original boxes/strips preferred, (4) previous medical reports, scans or prescriptions relevant to the visit, and (5) your insurance card if applicable. Arrive 15 minutes early to complete registration.

## What do I need to bring for a first-time visit?

On your first visit please bring a photo ID (NIC, passport or driving licence), your phone (for OTP verification), any past medical records or scans you have, a list of current medications, and your insurance card if you intend to claim. First-visit registration takes about 10 minutes; please arrive 15 minutes before your appointment time.

## Do I need to fast before a blood test?

Fasting is required for tests including Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS), Lipid Profile, and OGTT — fast for 8–12 hours, water is allowed. Postprandial Glucose (PPBS) requires eating exactly 2 hours before the draw. Most other blood tests do not require fasting; the lab counter will confirm at booking.

## How early should I arrive for my appointment?

Please arrive 15 minutes before your appointment time for OPD consultations, 30 minutes before for any imaging (CT, MRI, ultrasound), and 60 minutes before for procedures or day surgery. Earlier arrival lets us complete registration, vitals, and any pre-procedure paperwork without delaying the consultant.

## What should I bring for a CT or MRI scan?

Bring a photo ID, the doctor's referral request, any previous imaging on CD or report, and your appointment confirmation. Wear loose comfortable clothing without metal zips, hooks or embroidery. For contrast-enhanced scans, fast for 4 hours. Inform the radiographer if you have implants, a pacemaker, are pregnant, or have a known contrast allergy.

## Should I stop my medications before surgery?

Do NOT stop any prescription medications without your surgeon's instruction. Common pre-op guidance: continue regular medications with a sip of water; stop blood thinners (warfarin, aspirin, clopidogrel) only as advised, usually 5–7 days before surgery; hold metformin on the morning of surgery. Bring all current medications in their original packaging on the day of admission.

Our flagship Colombo facility is at 23 Sir Ernest De Silva Mawatha, Colombo 07. We also operate Nawaloka Hospital Negombo (84 Queen's Street, Negombo) and several OPD clinics in Greater Colombo. Use the 'list locations' command in chat or call 0114 544 444 for directions to a specific branch.

## How can I get to Nawaloka Hospital Colombo?

Nawaloka's main hospital is on Sir Ernest De Silva Mawatha, Colombo 07, beside the Liberty Plaza junction. By bus: routes 100, 138 and 170 stop within 200 m of the main entrance. By tuk/taxi: ask for 'Nawaloka Hospital, Sir Ernest De Silva Mawatha'. By private vehicle: parking is available on-site (see parking FAQ).

Yes — the main Colombo facility has a multi-level on-site car park accessible from the rear of Sir Ernest De Silva Mawatha. The first 30 minutes are complimentary for outpatients with a validated parking ticket; thereafter LKR 100 per hour. Valet parking is available at the main entrance for inpatient admissions and emergency arrivals.

Park in the multi-level on-site car park behind the main Colombo building, accessible from the rear of Sir Ernest De Silva Mawatha. The first 30 minutes are free with a validated outpatient ticket; LKR 100 per hour after that. Valet parking is available at the main entrance for inpatient admissions and emergency cases.

## Is parking free at Nawaloka?

Outpatients get the first 30 minutes free with a validated parking ticket from any clinic or counter. After that, parking is LKR 100 per hour at the on-site multi-level car park. Inpatients on admission and emergency arrivals can use complimentary valet parking at the main entrance.

## When do specialist clinics run?

Specialist consultant clinics at Nawaloka run by appointment between 14:00 and 21:00 daily. Each consultant has a fixed weekly schedule — for example cardiology Monday/Wednesday/Friday evenings, dermatology Tuesday/Thursday afternoons. Book through the call-centre on 0114 544 444 or the website to confirm a consultant's specific clinic days.

## What time do consultant clinics start?

Consultant clinics typically start at 14:00 and run until 21:00, by appointment only. A few morning clinics (07:00–10:00) operate for high-demand specialties such as paediatrics and obstetrics. Use the call-centre on 0114 544 444 or the booking website to find the start time for a specific consultant.

## Are consultants available in the morning?

Most specialist consultants run afternoon and evening clinics (14:00–21:00). Selected high-demand specialties — paediatrics, obstetrics, general medicine — also offer morning slots (07:00–10:00). Walk-in OPD doctors are available 07:00–22:00 every day for non-specialist consultations.

## What is the emergency hotline number?

For medical emergencies dial 0114 544 444 (24-hour ambulance and emergency desk) or 1990 for Suwa Seriya island-wide ambulance dispatch. The Nawaloka Emergency Department is open 24/7 with on-site triage, resuscitation, and admission capability.

## How do I call an ambulance?

Dial 0114 544 444 for the Nawaloka 24-hour ambulance dispatch (Greater Colombo), or 1990 for Suwa Seriya, the free island-wide pre-hospital service. Provide the location, the patient's age, and a brief description of the emergency. Stay on the line — the dispatcher will guide you on first aid until the ambulance arrives.

## What should I do in a medical emergency?

If the situation is life-threatening, dial 0114 544 444 (Nawaloka ambulance) or 1990 (Suwa Seriya) immediately. While waiting: keep the patient still, do not give food or water if unconscious or about to undergo surgery, note the time symptoms started, and gather any current medications to bring to the ER.

## What payment methods does Nawaloka accept?

We accept cash (LKR), all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AmEx), and direct bank transfers. We also accept payments through eZ Cash, FriMi, and Genie wallets. Inpatient final bills can be settled at the cashier on the ground floor before discharge.

## Do you accept my insurance?

Nawaloka has direct-billing arrangements with most major Sri Lankan insurance providers — AIA, Allianz, Janashakthi, Ceylinco, Softlogic, and Continental, among others. For approval, please bring your insurance card and a referral letter. Reimbursement-only cover (where you pay first and claim back) is supported for any other insurer; we provide a stamped detailed bill.

## How do I claim insurance for my appointment?

For direct-billing insurers: present your card at the registration desk before the consultation; we obtain pre-authorisation and you pay only your co-pay or non-covered items. For reimbursement claims: settle the bill at the cashier, request a detailed itemised receipt and the doctor's report, and submit them to your insurer. The billing office (ext. 215) can help with any insurer queries.

## What does an OPD consultation cost?

OPD consultation fees vary by specialist — typical channelling fees are LKR 3,500–6,500 per consultation, plus a hospital service charge of LKR 750. Fees for sub-specialty consultants and emergency consultations may be higher. Lab tests, scans, and procedures are billed separately at the published rates.

## How do I collect my lab or scan reports?

Routine lab reports are ready in 4 hours and can be collected from the lab counter or downloaded via the Nawaloka patient portal at portal.nawaloka.com using your booking ID and registered phone. CT and MRI reports take 12–24 hours; the radiologist's report is uploaded to the same portal. For STAT reports, please ask the consultant during the visit.

## Can I get my report online?

Yes — most lab and imaging reports are uploaded to portal.nawaloka.com within 4 hours of the test. Log in with your registered mobile number and booking ID to download a PDF copy. Hard copies are available at the respective collection counter free of charge.

## How do I refill a prescription?

Bring your previous prescription (or a photo of it) to the Nawaloka pharmacy on the ground floor — refills for non-controlled medications are issued the same day. Controlled drugs (psychiatric, opioid analgesics) require a fresh, dated prescription from the consultant. Prescriptions older than 30 days for routine medications also require revalidation by the prescribing doctor.

## Do you offer telemedicine or online consultations?

Yes — selected specialists offer video consultations via the Nawaloka Telehealth platform. Book through the website, the call-centre on 0114 544 444, or the in-app booking. You will receive a link 30 minutes before the consultation; please join 5 minutes early and have your reports ready as PDF or photo. Prescriptions are emailed and dispensed at any Nawaloka pharmacy.
