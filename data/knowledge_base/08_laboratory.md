# Laboratory Services and Pathology Guidelines

## What are the lab turnaround times?

Routine biochemistry and hematology results are available within 4 hours. Microbiology cultures take 48-72 hours. STAT test results are guaranteed within 1 hour.

## What are the blood specimen tube colours and their uses?

Purple (EDTA) for FBC, Light Blue (Citrate) for coagulation (PT/INR), Red (Serum) for biochemistry/immunology, and Grey (Fluoride/Oxalate) for glucose testing.

## What are the critical lab values that require immediate notification?

Critical values include Potassium < 2.5 or > 6.0 mmol/L, Glucose < 40 or > 400 mg/dL, and Platelets < 20,000. The lab MUST phone the clinical area within 15 minutes of verifying a critical result.

## How does the blood bank manage emergency blood requests?

For uncrossmatched emergency blood ('O Negative' protocol), 2 units can be issued within 5 minutes. Fully crossmatched blood requires 45-60 minutes.

## What quality control measures does the laboratory follow?

The laboratory runs daily internal QC samples before patient testing and participates in recognized External Quality Assurance (EQA) programs like RIQAS or RCPA.
