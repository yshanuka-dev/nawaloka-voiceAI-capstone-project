# Staff Training and Continuing Professional Development

## What is the new employee orientation programme?

All new hires undergo a 3-day general orientation covering hospital policies, safety protocols, infection control, and customer service, followed by a department-specific induction.

## What mandatory training is required annually?

Annual mandatory training includes Basic Life Support (BLS), Fire Safety and Evacuation, Infection Control, and Data Privacy updates.

## What CME requirements do doctors need to meet?

Consultants and Medical Officers must complete a minimum of 50 Continuing Medical Education (CME) credit hours per year to maintain their privileges at Nawaloka Hospital.

## What simulation training facilities are available?

The Clinical Training Centre features high-fidelity adult and pediatric manikins for Code Blue simulations, airway management training, and trauma resuscitation drills.

## What nursing specialisation courses does Nawaloka offer?

Through the Nursing Training School, we offer 6-month post-basic certification courses in Critical Care Nursing, Operating Theatre Technique, and Midwifery.
