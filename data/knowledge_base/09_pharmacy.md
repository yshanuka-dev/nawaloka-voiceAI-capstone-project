# Pharmacy Services and Medication Management

## What are the pharmacy operating hours?

The inpatient and outpatient pharmacies operate 24/7, including public holidays and weekends.

## How are controlled substances managed?

Controlled drugs (e.g., Morphine, Fentanyl) are kept in a double-locked safe. Administration requires a specific prescription, and a physical count is verified by two nurses at every shift change.

## What is the therapeutic drug monitoring process?

TDM involves drawing blood at specific times (peak or trough) to measure drug levels. Pharmacy adjusts dosing for narrow-therapeutic-index drugs like Vancomycin, Phenytoin, and Digoxin based on these results.

## How are cytotoxic drugs handled?

Cytotoxic drugs are prepared in a dedicated biological safety cabinet by trained oncology pharmacists wearing full protective gear, following strict hazardous drug protocols to prevent exposure.

## What drugs require TDM at Nawaloka?

Drugs requiring TDM at Nawaloka include Aminoglycosides (Gentamicin/Amikacin), Vancomycin, Digoxin, Lithium, Phenytoin, and immunosuppressants like Tacrolimus.
