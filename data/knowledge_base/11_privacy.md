# Patient Privacy and Data Protection Policies

## What is the data privacy policy?

Nawaloka Hospital complies with the Personal Data Protection Act. Patient records are confidential and only accessed by authorized staff directly involved in the patient's care.

## How is patient data classified?

Patient data is classified as Highly Sensitive Personal Health Information (PHI). It cannot be shared via unsecured platforms like WhatsApp or personal email.

## What is the break-the-glass procedure for EMR access?

In emergencies, clinicians can use the 'Break-the-Glass' feature to bypass access restrictions and view a patient's full EMR. This triggers an immediate audit log review by the Privacy Officer.

## How long are patient records retained?

Electronic medical records are kept indefinitely. Physical records for adults are retained for 10 years post-discharge; obstetric and pediatric records are retained for 21 years.

## What happens in case of a data breach?

Any suspected data breach must be reported to the IT Security Officer immediately. The hospital will contain the breach, notify affected patients within 72 hours, and report to regulatory authorities.
