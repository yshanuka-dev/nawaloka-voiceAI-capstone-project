# Staff and HR Policies

## What are the working hours and shift timings at Nawaloka Hospital?

Nawaloka Hospital clinical staff work in three main shifts: Morning (07:00-15:00), Evening (14:30-22:30), and Night (22:00-07:30). Administrative staff typically work Monday to Friday from 08:30 to 17:00.

## What is the leave policy for permanent employees?

Permanent employees are entitled to 14 days of Annual Leave, 21 days of Casual Leave/Sick Leave, and 84 working days of Maternity Leave. All leaves must be applied via the HRMS portal with at least 48 hours notice for planned absences.

## How many sick days do employees get?

Employees are entitled to 21 days of combined Casual and Sick Leave per annum. Medical certificates are required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

## What is the maternity leave entitlement?

Female employees are entitled to 84 working days (approx. 14 weeks) of fully paid Maternity Leave, excluding statutory holidays and weekends.

## What is the dress code for clinical staff?

Clinical staff must wear hospital-issued scrubs or uniforms with closed-toe, fluid-resistant shoes. Hair must be tied back, and dangling jewelry is prohibited. ID badges must be visible at all times.

## What are the employee medical benefits?

Employees receive free OPD consultations, subsidized indoor admissions, and comprehensive health insurance coverage up to LKR 1,000,000 per annum, including dependents.

## What is the disciplinary process at Nawaloka?

The disciplinary process involves verbal warnings, written warnings (up to three), suspension pending inquiry, and potential termination, depending on the severity of the misconduct as per the HR handbook.
