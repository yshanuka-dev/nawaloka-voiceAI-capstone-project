# Emergency Procedures and Code Protocols

## What is the Code Blue procedure for cardiac arrest?

Code Blue signifies a cardiac or respiratory arrest. Upon discovering an unresponsive patient, staff must call 5555, state 'Code Blue' and the location. Start CPR immediately. The resuscitation team aims to arrive within 3 minutes.

## What should staff do in case of a fire emergency?

In a fire emergency, activate the nearest fire alarm (Code Red). Use the RACE protocol: Rescue anyone in immediate danger, Alarm (call 4444), Contain the fire (close doors), and Extinguish/Evacuate.

## What is the Code Orange mass casualty protocol?

Code Orange is activated for external mass casualties. All off-duty ER staff are recalled, elective surgeries are canceled, and the triage area is expanded to the main lobby to handle the influx of patients.

## What happens during a power failure at the hospital?

In the event of a power failure, backup generators automatically start within 10 seconds. Critical equipment in ICUs and ORs are supported by UPS bridge power. Non-essential lighting will remain off.

## What are the fire extinguisher types and their uses?

Water extinguishers (Red) for combustible materials (wood, paper). CO2 extinguishers (Black) for electrical fires. Dry Powder (Blue) for flammable liquids and gases.

## What is the emergency evacuation procedure at the hospital?

The emergency evacuation procedure requires staff to calmly guide patients to the nearest exit stairwell (do not use elevators), assisting mobility-impaired patients using evacuation chairs. Assembly points are marked in the main car park.
