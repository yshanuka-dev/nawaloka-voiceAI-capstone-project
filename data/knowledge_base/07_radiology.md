# Radiology and Diagnostic Imaging Services

## What imaging equipment does Nawaloka have?

The radiology department is equipped with a 3 Tesla MRI, 128-slice CT scanner, Digital X-ray, Ultrasound with Doppler, Fluoroscopy, and a DEXA bone density scanner.

## What preparation is needed for an MRI scan?

Patients must complete a safety screening questionnaire. Metallic objects, jewelry, and credit cards must be removed. Fasting for 4-6 hours may be required if contrast is to be administered.

## What is the contrast reaction management protocol?

In the event of an allergic reaction to contrast media, the scan is immediately stopped. The radiologist administers epinephrine, antihistamines, and oxygen as per the anaphylaxis protocol. The crash cart is always on standby.

## What are the MRI safety contraindications?

Absolute contraindications for MRI include cardiac pacemakers (unless MRI-conditional), cochlear implants, certain aneurysm clips, and metallic fragments in the eyes.

## How long does it take to get a radiology report?

Routine X-ray and Ultrasound reports are finalized within 2-4 hours. CT and MRI reports take 12-24 hours. Urgent scans (STAT orders) are reported verbally to the physician within 30 minutes.
